Email notifications

Last updated October 21st, 2026

You can choose to opt in and out of receiving emails from Knox services that you manage. To manage your email notification settings:

  1. Click your account icon in the top-right, then click Settings.
  2. On the PERSONAL EMAIL PREFERENCES tab, select the service for which you want to manage email notifications.
  3. When you’re done, click SAVE to save your changes or X in the top-right to cancel them.

Manage email preferences

When configuring email preferences, choose the types of emails you want to receive from each service. The following tables describe the email options for each service:

MSP Portal

The MSP Portal allows Managed Service Providers to manage customer accounts and device uploads.

Email option Sub-options Description
Customer account management Add new and existing customers Receive emails when new and existing customers are added
Customer accepts or rejects management and delink requests Receive notifications when customers accept or reject management requests and delink requests
Reseller device uploads N/A Receive emails when a reseller uploads devices to the MSP portal
MSP Knox Guard permission changes N/A Receive emails when Samsung admin has given or revoked MSP permission to manage Knox Guard (only applicable if your company is using Knox Guard)

General Knox Service Settings

These settings apply across all Knox cloud services for your managed customers.

Email option Sub-options Description
Accounts N/A Emails about your account and other admin accounts in your company
Security lifecycle management alerts [Labs] N/A Recurring emails about the security lifecycle. Knox Mobile Enrollment, Knox E-FOTA, or Knox Asset Intelligence required
> Send recurring notification emails Select recurrence pattern

Admins are emailed a snapshot of the Insights section from the security dashboard for a selected period of time

Select a recurrence pattern from Weekly, Bi-weekly, Monthly, or Quarterly
> Send notification emails with conditions Devices past their security End-of-Life date

Admins are emailed when managed devices reach end-of-life

Select a recurrence pattern from Weekly, Bi-weekly, Monthly, or Quarterly
Devices with changes to their security patch cadence

Admins are emailed when managed devices have recent changes to their security patch cadence

Select a recurrence pattern from Weekly, Bi-weekly, Monthly, or Quarterly

Device deletions or removals Notifications when devices are removed
Licenses N/A License updates, renewal reminders, or seat count reminders
